# K. Houfek, T. N. Rescigno, C. W. McCurdy, Phys. Rev. A 73, 032721 (2006) — Numerically solvable model for resonant collisions of electrons with diatomic molecules

**Source:** `reference/literature/houfek-2006-pra73-032721.pdf` (gitignored) · DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.73.032721
**Pagination:** per-article, 032721-1 … 032721-12 (extractor page N = printed page 032721-N; offset zero, checked at page 1 and page 12).

## Why this repository cares

This is the paper that introduced the exact, non-Born-Oppenheimer 2-D
(one nuclear + one electronic coordinate) model of resonant electron-diatomic
collisions that `qscat.core`, `qscat.dvr`, `qscat.ecs` and `qscat.model` exist to
solve. Every piece of vocabulary the repo uses — the driven Lippmann-Schwinger
equation, the FEM-DVR-ECS numerical method, the VE/DA T-matrix and
cross-section definitions, the local-complex-potential (LCP/"boomerang")
reduction under test, and the N₂-like/NO-like Morse+sigmoid potential
parametrization — is defined here first. Without this source, `qscat.model.N2`
and `qscat.model.NO`'s parameters, and the LCP-vs-exact comparison that is
this repo's central research question, would be unfounded numbers.

## Equations

```
H = T_R + T_r + V(R,r)                                          p. 032721-2, Eq. (3)
T_R = -(1/2mu) d^2/dR^2,  T_r = -(1/2) d^2/dr^2                  p. 032721-2, Eq. (4)
V(R,r) = V0(R) + l(l+1)/(2 r^2) + V_int(R,r)                     p. 032721-2, Eq. (5)
V_int(R,r) = -lambda(R) exp(-alpha(R) r^2)                       p. 032721-2, Eq. (6)
H_el = T_r + V(R,r)                                              p. 032721-2, Eq. (8)
[T_R + V0(R)] chi_v(R) = E_v chi_v(R)                            p. 032721-2, Eq. (11)
(E - H) Psi_sc(R,r) = V_int(R,r) Psi0_vi(R,r)                    p. 032721-2, Eq. (14)
Psi_sc --r->inf--> sum_vf f_VE_{vi->vf} chi_vf(R) r h_l^(1)(k_ef r)   p. 032721-2, Eq. (15)
Psi_sc --R->inf--> f_DA_vi phi_b(r) R h_0^(1)(K_DA R)            p. 032721-3, Eq. (16)
T_VE_{vi->vf}(E) = <Psi0_vf|V_VE|Psi_E^+> = f_VE/(2 k_ef)        p. 032721-3, Eq. (19)
T_DA_vi(E) = <Psi0_DA|V_DA|Psi_E^+> = f_DA/(2 mu K_DA)           p. 032721-3, Eq. (20)
V_DA(R,r) = V0(R) + V_int(R,r) - lim_{R->inf} V_int(R,r)         p. 032721-3, Eq. (24)
sigma_VE_{vi->vf}(E) = 16 pi (k_ef/k_ei) |T_VE_{vi->vf}(E)|^2    p. 032721-3, Eq. (25)
sigma_DA_vi(E)       = 16 pi (mu K_DA/k_ei) |T_DA_vi(E)|^2       p. 032721-3, Eq. (26)
R'(R) = R for R<R0; R0+(R-R0)e^{i theta_R} for R>=R0             p. 032721-3, Eq. (27)
r'(r) = r for r<r0; r0+(r-r0)e^{i theta_r} for r>=r0             p. 032721-3, Eq. (28)
H_el(R) phi(r;R) = V_res(R) phi(r;R)                             p. 032721-5, Eq. (36)
V_res(R) = E_res(R) - (i/2) Gamma(R)                             p. 032721-5, Eq. (37)
[E - T_R - V_res(R)] xi_E(R) = eta_vi(R) chi_vi(R)               p. 032721-5, Eq. (38)
sigma_VE_{vi->vf}(E) = (4 pi^3/k_ei^2) |Int chi_vf* eta_vf xi_E dR|^2   p. 032721-5, Eq. (39)
sigma_DA_vi(E) = (2 pi^2/k_ei^2)(K_DA/mu) lim_{R->inf} |xi_E(R)|^2      p. 032721-5, Eq. (40)
eta_v(R) = sqrt(Gamma(R)/2pi)                                    p. 032721-5, Eq. (41)
eta_v(R) = gamma_v^{l+1/2}(R) sqrt(Gamma(R)/2pi)  [barrier-penetration factor]  p. 032721-5, Eq. (42)
gamma_v(R) = k_v/k(R) if k_v<k(R), else 1                        p. 032721-5, Eq. (43)
k(R)^2/2 = E_res(R) - V0(R)                                      p. 032721-5, Eq. (44)
V0(R) = D0( e^{-2 alpha0 (R-R0)} - 2 e^{-alpha0 (R-R0)} )         p. 032721-6, Eq. (45)
lambda(R) = lambda_inf + lambda0 / (1 + e^{lambda1 (R-R_lambda)})   p. 032721-6, Eq. (46)
lambda0 = (lambda_c - lambda_inf)(1 + e^{lambda1 (R_c - R_lambda)}) p. 032721-6, Eq. (47)
alpha(R) = alpha_c                                                p. 032721-6, Eq. (48)
```

Atomic units throughout (energies in hartree, lengths in bohr) — the same
convention `libs/qscat/qscat/units.py` uses; no conversion needed.

## Findings and limits

- **N₂**: the exact 2-D cross sections are qualitatively and quantitatively
  close to the physical e⁻+N₂ system. The LCP approximation is nearly exact
  for low final vibrational states (v'=1,2) but fails badly for v'=8, where
  the dynamics probe the anion/neutral crossing region — p. 032721-9.
- **Elastic (v'=0) scattering**: LCP fails at low energy from incorrect
  threshold behavior (diverges as k_ei→0); the barrier-penetration factor
  (Eq. 42-44) fixes the first two peaks but *worsens* agreement for every
  inelastic channel, and neither LCP variant captures the non-resonant
  background contribution to the elastic cross section — p. 032721-9.
- **NO**: because the anion/neutral crossing sits near the neutral
  potential's minimum, LCP agreement is generally worse than for N₂, for
  both VE and DA — p. 032721-9 – 032721-10.
- **NO dissociative attachment**: LCP overestimates by orders of magnitude
  for low initial vibrational states v_i (vertical-transition assumption
  breaks down), is quite good for v_i≈10–15, and degrades again for
  v_i≳15 near threshold. The barrier-penetration factor uniformly worsens
  DA agreement too — p. 032721-10.
- The paper states its own scope limit: this is the *first* of a planned
  series (p. 032721-11, "will be reported in a subsequent study") comparing
  the exact model only against the LCP/boomerang reduction, not against
  nonlocal theories — though it sketches (Eq. 49-50) how a Feshbach
  projection operator could be built in the same FEM-DVR representation for
  that future comparison.
- The paper's own Fig. 6-8 are plots only; no cross-section values are
  tabulated in the PDF. The golden numeric dataset this repo gates against
  (`validation/n2/data/CSVE.V00.J00`, loaded by `validation/n2/loader.py`) is
  a separate data source, not extracted from this PDF — see that loader's
  own docstring/provenance rather than this note for its origin.

## Not used here

- The Feshbach `Q(R)` projection-operator construction (Eq. 49-50) and the
  broader program of comparing against nonlocal theories (Sec. VI) — this
  repo compares the exact model only against the LCP reduction, matching
  this paper's own scope, not its stated future direction.
- The literal entry/exit-amplitude LCP formulation (Eq. 38-44, including the
  barrier-penetration factor `gamma_v(R)`) is not transcribed as code:
  `qscat.core.lcp.lcp_da_cross_section` uses a boundary-wavefunction-value
  flux formulation instead (see `docs/physics/lcp-da-approximation` and the
  `lcp-da-approximation` memory) — a different but related route to the same
  LCP approximation this paper defines.
- Fig. 2-8 (potential surfaces, wave-function plots, cross-section
  comparison curves) are read for their qualitative conclusions only; no
  pixel/curve data was extracted from them.
- The `1050-2947/2006/73(3)/032721(12)` APS boilerplate, author affiliations,
  and acknowledgments carry no technical content for this repo.
