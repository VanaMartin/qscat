## Why this repository cares

This is the paper that supplies the exact 2-D benchmark for `qscat.model.H2P`
and `qscat.core.dr_cross_section`: the same one-electronic + one-nuclear-coordinate
FEM-DVR-ECS model the N₂/NO/F₂ papers use, generalized to a charged target
(H₂⁺, `-1/r` Coulomb tail, Rydberg exit channels) — see `docs/physics/h2plus-dr.md`.
It is also the third independent published source (after Hvizdoš's 2016 thesis
and Váňa's 2017 thesis) for the H₂⁺ reduced mass, which corrected a real error
inherited from the eMoScat deck (918.25 → 918.076). Separately, its Appendix A
defines *Siegert pseudostates* — a construction this repo does not implement but
must not be confused with: `docs/physics/lcp-resonance-levels.md` §3 draws an
explicit terminology contrast against it for the ECS complex-scaled resonance
eigenstates `qscat.core.lcp.ResonanceLevels` actually computes.

H(R,r) = H0(R,r) + V(R,r) = H_N(R) + H_e(r) + V(R,r)             p. 4, Eq. (4)
H_N = -(1/2M) d^2/dR^2 + V0(R)                                    p. 4, Eq. (5)
H_e = -(1/2) d^2/dr^2 - 1/r + l(l+1)/(2 r^2)                      p. 4, Eq. (6)
V0(R) = D0( e^{-2 alpha0 (R-Re)} - 2 e^{-alpha0 (R-Re)} )         p. 4, Eq. (7)
  D0 = 0.1027 Hartree, alpha0 = 0.69 Bohr^-1, Re = 2.0 Bohr; M = 918.076 a.u.
V(R,r) = -alpha1 (1 - tanh((alpha2-R-alpha3 R^4)/7)) tanh^4(R/alpha4) e^{-r^2/3}/r   p. 4, Eq. (8)

psi+_E = psi_in + psi_sc,  (E-H0) psi_in = 0                     p. 4, Eq. (10)-(11)
(E-H) psi_sc(R,r) = V(R,r) psi_in(R,r)                            p. 4, Eq. (12)
psi_in(R,r) = chi_j'(R) Phi_{k_j',l}(r)                           p. 5, Eq. (13)

H_e(r) Phi_{k,l}(r) = (k^2/2) Phi_{k,l}(r)                        p. 5, Eq. (15)
psi_sc --R->inf--> sqrt(2/(pi k_j')) sum_n f^DR_{j'->n} rho_n(r) e^{i K_n R}   p. 5, Eq. (16)
[H_e + V_inf(r)] rho_n(r) = E_n rho_n(r),  V_inf(r) = lim_{R->inf} V(R,r)     p. 5, Eq. (17)

## Findings and limits

- The FT-with-Siegert-pseudostates approach agrees well with the exact 2-D
  model's DR cross sections across 0-2 eV, except in narrow energy windows
  just below each vibrational threshold — p. 21-22 (§IV).
- Those windows are a stated *limitation of the exact 2-D calculation itself*,
  not of FT: a finite electronic grid can only represent a finite number of
  Rydberg states, so cross sections just below each vibrational threshold
  (where an infinite Rydberg series should accumulate) are not converged —
  p. 6-7 (§II B). About 20 Rydberg states are well represented on the
  Table I grid.
- The nuclear/electronic ECS bending-angle bounds (θ < π/8, θ < π/4) are
  stated as a consequence of the coupling potential `V(R,r)`'s form (Eq. 8):
  the `α3 R^4` term diverges under rotation past π/8, and the electronic
  `e^{-r²/3}` factor diverges past π/4 — p. 6 (§II B).
- The model potential (Eq. 8) is noted to support an unphysical `n=1, l=1`
  p-wave state as `R→∞` at asymptotic energy −37.5 eV, a known artifact of
  the model rather than a physical H₂⁺/H+H(1s) feature — p. 8 (Fig. 1
  caption context, prose).
- The paper's own scope limit: FT here neglects the energy dependence of the
  quantum defect and uses the Born-Oppenheimer approximation at short range
  (the source of the residual FT-vs-exact discrepancies); an energy-dependent
  frame transformation is left to future work — p. 22 (§IV, end).
- No cross-section values are tabulated in the PDF (Figs. 1-6 are plots
  only); this repo's own H₂⁺ σ_DR(E) curve is validated against its own
  exact 2-D solve (`docs/physics/h2plus-dr.md`), not extracted from this
  source.

## Not used here

- §III (Frame Transformation) and its full machinery — the FT-into-Siegert-
  pseudostates approximation this paper tests, including the S-matrix frame
  transformation (Eq. 36), the surface-corrected FT formula (Eq. 2), and the
  subunitarity/missing-flux DR-rate argument (§III B, Eq. 42). This repo
  solves the exact 2-D model directly (`qscat.core.dr_cross_section`); it
  does not implement or compare against the approximate FT route this paper
  benchmarks.
- Appendix A in full (the Siegert pseudostate basis expansion, functional
  `F_j[.]`, and the derivation of the surface-corrected orthogonality
  relation) beyond Eq. (A3)-(A5) — read for terminology contrast only
  (`docs/physics/lcp-resonance-levels.md` §3), not as a construction to
  reproduce.
- Appendix B and any remaining appendices, and the full derivation chain in
  §III leading to the channel-coupling potential `V_jm(R)` (referenced at
  p. 5, "the exact form ... can be found in the Appendix").
- Figs. 1-6 (cross-section comparison plots) are read for their qualitative
  conclusions only; no pixel/curve data was extracted from them.
- Author affiliations, acknowledgments, and the bibliography carry no
  technical content for this repo (its own citations are independently
  tracked in `reference/literature/README.md`).
