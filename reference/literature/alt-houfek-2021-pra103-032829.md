## Why this repository cares

This is the **first real-molecule target of the potential factory** (`docs/physics/potential-factory.md`, `validation/factory/targets/o2.py`). It is the one published nonlocal resonance model whose construction is complete on the page: *ab initio* curves for the neutral and the anion, R-matrix eigenphase sums at fixed `R`, and a closed-form energy-dependent width `Γ̃(ε, R)` with all seven fitted constants tabulated. That is exactly the tier-T3 data format the factory consumes, and it comes from the supervisor's own group. Without this source the O₂ target would have no anchor: no curves, no width function, no threshold exponent, no electron affinity to constrain the asymptote.

## Equations

Atomic units throughout the paper (the repo's units). Transcribed in the repo's docstring notation.

```
[E − T_R − V_d(R)] ψ_d(R) − ∫dR' F(E,R,R') ψ_d(R') = V_dε_i(R) χ_v_i(R)        p. 032829-2, Eq. (3)
F(E,R,R') = Δ(E,R,R') − (i/2) Γ(E,R,R')                                          p. 032829-2, Eq. (4)
Γ̃(ε,R,R') = 2π V_dε(R) V_dε*(R')                                                p. 032829-2, Eq. (7)
Γ(R) = 2π |V_dE_res(R)(R)|²      (local width)                                   p. 032829-2, Eq. (12)
V_loc(R) = V_ion(R) − (i/2) Γ(R) = V_d(R) + Δ(R) − (i/2) Γ(R)                    p. 032829-2, Eq. (15)
K(ε,R) = tan δ_sum(ε,R) = (Γ(R)/2) / (E_res(R) − ε)                              p. 032829-4, Eq. (20)
V_ion(R) = V_0(R) + E_res(R)                                                     p. 032829-4, Eq. (21)
δ(ε,R) = δ_bg(ε,R) + δ_res(ε,R)                                                  p. 032829-4, Eq. (22)
δ_res  = −arctan[ (Γ̃(ε,R)/2) / (ε − V_d(R) − Δ̃(ε,R) + V_0(R)) ]                 p. 032829-4, Eq. (23)
δ_bg   = c ε^α                                                                   p. 032829-4, Eq. (24)
Γ̃(ε,R) = 2π ε^α A(R) exp(−B(R) ε),  α = l + 1/2, l = 2                         p. 032829-4, Eq. (25)
A(R) = (a0 + a1 R) exp(a2 R)                                                     p. 032829-4, Eq. (26)
B(R) = b0 + b1 R                                                                 p. 032829-4, Eq. (27)
Δ̃(ε,R) = (1/2π) P.V. ∫dε' Γ̃(ε',R)/(ε − ε')                                      p. 032829-4, Eq. (28)
V_d(R) = V_0(R) + E_res(R) − Δ̃(E_res(R), R)                                     p. 032829-4, Eq. (29)
Γ̃(ε,R,R') = sqrt(Γ̃(ε,R) Γ̃(ε,R'))                                              p. 032829-4, Eq. (30)
```

The VE and DA cross sections (Eqs. (9)–(10), p. 032829-2) carry the statistical factor `g` and `σ_DA = (2π²/k_i²)(K/μ) lim_{R→∞}|ψ_d(R)|²`; the repo's own exact-2-D normalisation is anchored elsewhere (`houfek-2006-pra73-032721.md`), not here.

## Findings and limits

- The ²Π_g curve is bound at large `R` and becomes a resonance at short `R`; the crossing with the ³Σ_g⁻ curve is near the neutral's equilibrium, so the resonance region is `R ≲ 2.3 a₀` (p. 032829-3, Fig. 2; the extracted crossing is 2.289 a₀).
- The curves are *ab initio* with the anion shifted to the experimental EA(O); the authors accept a possibly slight misplacement of the narrow VE peaks for the correct DA threshold (p. 032829-3, §III A).
- The nonlocal model's width function is the closed-form Eq. (25) fitted to R-matrix eigenphase sums over `R = 1.80–2.25 a₀` (Fig. 3, p. 032829-5); outside that window Eqs. (26)–(27) are an extrapolation.
- LCP cross sections for inelastic VE are much larger than the nonlocal ones at low energy because of the local width's wrong threshold behaviour (p. 032829-5, §IV A) — the same qualitative LCP failure this repo measures on its own models.
