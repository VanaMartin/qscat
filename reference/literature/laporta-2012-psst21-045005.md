## Why this repository cares

This is a low-energy CO shape-resonance calculation with an explicit SEP
prescription and a geometry-dependent resonance curve. It motivates the
six-electron polarization check in `projects/ukrmol_co`, and explains why an
RHF scattering threshold should be replaced by a better neutral curve when
constructing nuclear dynamics. Its empirical width adjustment is relevant
when assessing agreement with experiment.

## Findings and limits

SEP resonance parameters are sensitive to the model choices (p. 4).
The width and level shift are empirically adjusted (pp. 4, 6), so agreement of the final
vibrational cross sections is not independent evidence of a converged
unadjusted fixed-nuclei width. Short-R widths are also fit-sensitive (p. 3;
p. 7, Fig. 2).
The paper's electronic calculation uses a different diatomic implementation
and basis from the Gaussian UKRmol+ pilot (p. 3).

## Terminology map

- Internuclear distance → `bond_length`, in bohr (pp. 3, 5).
- Fixed-nuclei resonance energy relative to the neutral threshold →
  `native_resonances[*].energy_hartree`; published eV are converted at the
  boundary (pp. 4–5).
- Gamma → `width_hartree`, the full resonance width (p. 4).

## Not used here

The LCP nuclear dynamics, high-vibrational-state rates, rotational study,
Morse parametrization, and the empirical width correction.
