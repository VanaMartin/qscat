   could change that choice; report numerical versus electronic uncertainties.
2. Demonstrate the chosen fitting-information route, including width/potential
   identifiability limits and held-out scattering predictions.
3. Establish state/resonance continuity and an adequate neutral reference over
   the chosen pilot domain.
4. Run a small restartable geometry pilot with reserved validation data and a
   measured time/memory/disk forecast. Then decide on the larger sweep.

The [goal-convergence checkpoint](PRODUCTION_DECISIONS.md#goal-convergence-checkpoint--every-one-or-two-days-of-campaign-work)
is the stopping/redirect authority at each cycle, not the next technical item in
an old branch's handoff. Earlier scientific failures remain unchanged.
