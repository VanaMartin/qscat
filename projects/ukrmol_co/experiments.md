[campaign lessons](../../docs/physics/co-experiment-lessons.md). Experiment plans,
observations, source capsules and continuation decisions are immutable external
records, not files to copy back into this catalogue. Add only an ID, scope and
content-addressed record reference. Existing campaign data await separate cleanup.
