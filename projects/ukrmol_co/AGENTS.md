# CO campaign goal checks

Follow the repository operating manual. Before launching or extending a CO
experiment, read the current goal-convergence checkpoint and disposition in
`PRODUCTION_DECISIONS.md`, plus `POTENTIAL_ROUTE_DECISION.md`. Historical "next"
items in evidence notes do not override the current campaign decision.

Apply the goal-convergence checklist every one or two days of active work, on
resuming after a gap and before opening a new experimental branch. Record the
production decision/physical uncertainty addressed, minimum reused-data scope,
compute and implementation-effort bounds, evidence delta and continue/redirect/
stop decision. More passing tests or interface checks alone are not sweep progress.
Update `CONTINUATION.md` with the checkpoint and one bounded next deliverable.
This cadence is a working rule, not authorization for unattended jobs or new
calculations; existing scientific gates and execution contracts still apply.
